Two Marches to Nationhood

A parallel thematic timeline comparing George Washington’s 1775–1776 campaign from Boston to the Delaware River with the Biblical Exodus from Egypt to Mount Sinai.

Revolutionary War (Boston → Delaware)
The Exodus (Egypt → Mount Sinai)
Phase I: Breaking the Grip of Empire
Apr 1775 – Mar 17, 1776

Siege & Evacuation of Boston

Following Lexington and Concord, colonial militias besiege British-held Boston. Placing cannons from Fort Ticonderoga onto Dorchester Heights forces the British fleet to evacuate the city.

1
Exodus 12 • Day 1

Passover & Departure from Rameses

After ten devastating plagues culminate in the first Passover, Pharaoh relents. Moses leads the Israelites out of Egypt, guided through the desert by a pillar of cloud and fire.

Phase II: Miraculous Escape Across Water
August 29–30, 1776

Night Evacuation of Brooklyn

Defeated at the Battle of Long Island and pinned against the East River, Washington uses Marblehead mariners and a dense morning fog to ferry 9,000 troops to Manhattan undetected.

2
Exodus 14 • Days 4–7

Crossing the Red Sea

Trapped between Pharaoh's advancing chariots and the sea at Pi-hahiroth, Moses stretches his hand over the waters. Israel crosses on dry ground before the returning waves destroy their pursuers.

Phase III: Trials in the Wilderness
Nov – Early Dec 1776

Retreat Across New Jersey

Driven out of New York after losing Forts Washington and Lee, the shrinking Continental Army retreats across New Jersey in freezing rain, lacking shoes, rations, and winter coats.

3
Exodus 15–16 • Weeks 2–6

Wilderness of Shur & Sin

Exhausted and thirsty in the desert, the Israelites despair over scarce food and water. They are sustained by bitter water made sweet at Marah, manna from heaven, and water from a rock.

Phase IV: Tested at the Brink
December 1776

"The Times That Try Men's Souls"

Encamped across the icy Delaware River in Pennsylvania, enlistments are days from expiring and desertions mount. Thomas Paine writes The American Crisis to rally fading morale.

4
Exodus 17 • Rephidim

Battle Against the Amalekites

Weakened from travel before reaching Sinai, Israel is ambushed by Amalek at Rephidim. Joshua leads the defense in the valley while Aaron and Hur hold up Moses's arms until victory.

Phase V: The Defining Covenant & Victory
December 25–26, 1776

Crossing the Delaware & Trenton

On Christmas night, Washington crosses the ice-choked Delaware River in a nor'easter and captures the Hessian garrison at Trenton, rescuing the American Revolution from collapse.

5
Exodus 19–20 • Month 3

Arrival & Law at Mount Sinai

Encamping at the base of Mount Sinai amid thunder and smoke, Moses ascends the mountain to receive the Ten Commandments—transforming former slaves into a covenanted nation.
